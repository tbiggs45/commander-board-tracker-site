Commander Board Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Commander Board Tracker doesn’t collect your data. There’s no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your games, decks and card edits stay on your iPhone. The app goes online only to look up cards on Scryfall, and those lookups carry the text being looked up and nothing that identifies you.

What stays on your iPhone

The app saves these on your iPhone so it can work offline and resume a game:

  • Your current game: the board, life, counters, commander damage, the game log, and the opponent names and life totals you enter.
  • The name you type on New Game, so it’s filled in next time, and your Appearance choice.
  • Card text you edit or refresh from Scryfall.
  • Deck files you add to the app’s folder in the Files app.

None of this is sent to the developer or anyone else. Like other app data, it’s included in your iPhone’s iCloud or computer backups. Starting a new game replaces the saved one, and deleting the app deletes all of it.

What goes to Scryfall

Scryfall is a free Magic: The Gathering card database. It isn’t run by the developer of this app. The app contacts Scryfall only to:

  • search for a card you’re adding, sending what you typed;
  • show a card’s image or refresh its text, sending the card’s name (images then load from Scryfall’s image servers);
  • identify a card from a photo, sending the text read from the top of the photo, which is usually the card’s name. It sends one line at a time, at most four, and stops at the first match. If you correct the name, it sends your correction.

These requests contain no account, name or device identifier. Apart from the text being looked up, they only say they come from CommanderBoardTracker/1.0 (iOS). Like any website, Scryfall sees your IP address when your iPhone connects to it; Scryfall’s privacy policy covers what it does with that. Everything else in the app works offline.

Camera and photos

  • The camera is used only when you take a photo of a card to add it. If you choose a photo instead, iOS shows its own photo picker and the app receives only the one photo you pick. It can’t see the rest of your library.
  • The photo is read on your iPhone with Apple’s built-in text recognition. It isn’t saved and isn’t uploaded. Only the text described above goes to Scryfall.

What the app doesn’t do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics, crash reporting or other third-party code.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information. The developer never receives any.

Apple, TestFlight and the App Store

Apple runs TestFlight and the App Store, and Apple’s privacy policy covers the data Apple handles. Apple shares some of it with the developer in two cases:

  • TestFlight testers. TestFlight shows the developer the name and email you were invited with (if you were invited by email), basic usage such as installs, sessions and crashes, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send.
  • Crash and usage data. If you turned on Share With App Developers (Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements), Apple shares crash reports and usage statistics with the developer.

The developer uses this only to find and fix problems in the app.

Children

The app doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Everything the app keeps is on your iPhone and under your control. Start a new game to replace the saved one, or delete the app to remove all of its data. The developer holds none of your data, so there’s nothing to request or delete on the developer’s side. If you’d rather not contact Scryfall at all, use the app offline: tracking a game doesn’t need a connection.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, for example by syncing games between phones, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Testers can reach the developer with Send Beta Feedback in the TestFlight app.
